Pool costs in Toowoomba: what affects the price

In short: We don't publish our own pool prices. Published industry guides give broad national ranges, and a local builder has to quote your block.

Use the ranges only as a starting point. What “installed” includes varies, and fencing, extra earthworks and landscaping are often extra.

Last updated: 23 November 2026

We don't set or advertise our own pool prices. The figures below come from published industry guides, with links. They are national or state guides, not quotes for Toowoomba, and often exclude fencing, extra excavation or landscaping. A local builder has to price your block.

Published industry ranges

  • Poolpages' pool installation cost guide (updated November 2026) says about A$45,000–85,000 installed for fibreglass and A$55,000–110,000 or more for concrete, before landscaping.
  • What's The Damage's fibreglass vs concrete guide gives similar installed bands: fibreglass about A$50,000–85,000, concrete about A$55,000–110,000, and premium concrete with a spa and heating A$120,000–220,000, GST included.
  • Summertime Pools' 2026 fibreglass guide puts a 6–7 m family pool in Queensland at about A$53,000–68,000 installed, before surrounds.

Guides disagree, and what “installed” includes varies. Use them only as a starting point, then get a written quote for your site.

What usually changes the quote

Type and size

Concrete, fibreglass or plunge; the length, width and depth; and extras such as a spa, waterline tiles, lighting or a cleaner.

The site

Suburbs along the eastern escarpment, such as Rangeville and Mount Lofty, are hilly, and reactive clay soils are common across the Downs. Slope, rock, reactive soil, drainage and machine access can add earthworks, retaining or engineering.

Fencing, paving and landscaping

A compliant pool barrier is required by law. Paving, planting, lighting and outdoor areas are often quoted separately from the shell, so confirm in writing what's in and out.

Heating and covers

With a shorter swimming season in Toowoomba, many owners ask about solar or heat-pump heating and a cover. These are often priced separately, so ask for them as line items.

Approvals

Approvals, certification, inspections and, where needed, engineering are part of a new pool. See our QLD fencing & approvals guide.

About the budget field on our form

The budget bands on the form are for you to show a range if you have one. They are not a price list and not a promise that a pool can be built for that amount on your site.
