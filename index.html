Building a pool in Toowoomba

Toowoomba's height on the range changes the conversation about pools. Nights are cooler and winters are frosty, so heating, a cover and the position of the pool for sun are worth raising with the builder. The swimming season is shorter than on the coast, but a heated pool extends it.

Ground conditions matter too. Reactive clay soils are common on the Downs, and escarpment suburbs such as Rangeville and Mount Lofty can be steep or rocky. A builder will look at soil and slope before recommending concrete or fibreglass.

How much does a pool cost in Toowoomba?

It depends on the type, size, site, access, fencing and what's included. We don't publish our own prices. Industry guides linked in our pool cost guide give national ranges only. A local builder has to quote your block.

Do I need approval for a new pool?

Yes. The QBCC and Queensland Government say a new pool needs building approval and must comply with the pool safety laws. The builder usually engages a private certifier. See our QLD fencing and approvals guide.

Do I have to register the pool?

Yes. All regulated pools in Queensland must be registered with the QBCC.

What's the difference between concrete, fibreglass and plunge pools?

Concrete is built on site and can be custom shaped. Fibreglass is a pre-formed shell that is delivered and installed. A plunge pool is a smaller pool in either material. See our pool types guide.
